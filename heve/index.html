Hands, eyes, voice
and ears.

HEVE is SeKondBrain's multimodal layer, in one place. It is four capabilities: Dom Sentinel reads the live web, Browser Control acts on it, Kora Voice speaks and listens, and Diarisation knows who is talking.

Where memory, the graph and reasoning give an agent a mind, HEVE gives it a body — a way to perceive the web and a room, to act, and to be heard.

Dom Sentinel
— its eyes

A browser-side engine that reads the live web — and heals itself when it changes. You describe the elements you care about by intent, each with an ordered chain of fallback selectors. Sentinel watches the page; when a site ships new markup and your selectors break, it captures a snapshot and a screenshot, asks a vision model for replacements, and hot-swaps them. No new build, no broken extractor.

Browser Control
— its hands

Reading a page is half of it; HEVE can also act on one. Browser Control drives the browser the way a person would — opening and navigating pages, scrolling through long or virtualised feeds until they settle, and clicking through multi-step task interfaces — so an agent can operate a web app end to end, not only observe it. It runs on the same self-healing selector engine as Sentinel, so the actions survive the site shifting underneath them.

Kora Voice
— its voice

A full spoken-conversation layer: speech-to-text, text-to-speech, voice-activity and break detection, and a conversation state machine behind a drop-in UI. It knows when you have finished a sentence, replies out loud, and handles being interrupted — talking to it should feel like talking to a presence, not operating a debug overlay.

Diarisation
— its ears

Voice tells you what was said; diarisation tells you who said it. Enrol a speaker once and HEVE recognises them in real time in a room of several people — keeping per-person context and permissions, and greeting each by name. It is what turns a single-user voice assistant into one that works around a table.

Your data

On the web (Sentinel & Browser Control). HEVE reads and acts only on the pages you enable it on, and only to do the job you asked. During a self-heal, a snapshot and screenshot of that page are sent to the vision provider you configure — so on a page holding private content, that content can reach the model provider you chose. You hold the provider key, and you hold the kill-switch.

Voice is biometric data

In the room (Voice & Diarisation). HEVE captures microphone audio, and diarisation creates a voiceprint — a biometric identifier, treated as special-category personal data. Enrolment is explicit; raw audio is not retained; voice signatures are encrypted at rest. If you choose a cloud transcription provider, audio is sent to it. Handling, consent and deletion are set out in the privacy policy.
