30-day money-back guarantee

Individuals on paid plans — Terms of Service clause 4.3A

If you are not satisfied with a paid subscription, you may request a full refund within 30 days of your initial purchase of that subscription. You do not need to give a reason.

This sits on top of your statutory rights and does not limit them. Three conditions apply:

  • it applies once per subscription;
  • it applies to the initial purchase only, not to renewal payments; and
  • the refund is returned to your original payment method, issued by us — or, where a refund to that method is no longer possible, by another means we agree with you (see below).

14-day consumer cooling-off

Consumers only — Terms of Service clause 4.3

If you are a Consumer — acting for purposes wholly or mainly outside your trade, business, craft or profession — you have 14 days from purchase to cancel and receive a refund under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

Many digital services ask you to waive that right at checkout so they can start supplying immediately. We do not. You get access straight away and you keep the full 14 days, and if you cancel inside them we refund you in full — we do not deduct anything for the days you used. You do not need to give a reason.

In practice

Because we never ask you to waive it, the 14-day right is always available to you. For an initial purchase the 30-day money-back guarantee above is simply the longer window, so that is usually the one to use. The 14-day right is what matters on a renewal, which the 30-day guarantee does not cover.

Each time a subscription renews automatically, you have a fresh 14-day right to cancel that renewal and be refunded in full for it. This applies to every renewal, on every plan, wherever you are — it is not limited to annual plans or to the UK (clause 4.3D).

Cancelling without a refund

Terms of Service clauses 4.1, 4.4 and 4.5

Outside the windows above, you may cancel at any time with effect from the end of your current billing period. You keep access until that period ends. Except as stated in the Terms or required by law, fees already paid are non-refundable and we do not pro-rate a part-used period.

Subscriptions renew automatically for successive periods equal to the initial period unless you cancel before renewal. We remind Consumers of upcoming renewals where the law requires it.

If a payment fails, we may suspend paid features after giving you notice and a reasonable opportunity to update your payment method.

Business customers

Organisation plans — Business Terms clauses 8.1, 8.3 and 9.3

There is no cooling-off period and no money-back guarantee on organisation plans. The 30-day guarantee and the 14-day consumer right above apply to individual and self-serve accounts, not to customers contracting on the Business Terms.

Fees stated in the Order are non-refundable except as expressly stated in the agreement. Subscription fees are invoiced annually or monthly in advance, usage-based fees in arrears, invoices are payable within 30 days, and seats added mid-term are charged pro rata.

Renewal pricing may change with at least 60 days’ notice before the renewal date. Either party may give notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the current term. On expiry or termination you have 30 days to export your data using the export tools or by requesting a machine-readable export.

Query a charge with us before raising a chargeback. Where an organisation customer raises one and it is not resolved in their favour, the dispute fees charged to us are reimbursable and may be set off against sums owing (Business Terms 8.2A).

How to request a refund

Email support@sekondbrain.ai from the address on your account, telling us which subscription you mean and, if you are relying on the 30-day guarantee, that you are doing so. You do not need to explain why.

We aim to acknowledge refund requests within two working days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method — or, where a refund to that method is no longer possible (an expired card, a closed account), by another means we agree with you. How long the money takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

If you don’t recognise a charge

Terms of Service clause 4.5A

Come to us first. Email support@sekondbrain.ai and we will identify the charge and, if it should not have been taken, refund it — usually faster than your bank can. We aim to acknowledge within two working days.

Charges appear on your statement as statement descriptor pending.

If you raise a chargeback with your bank or card issuer, we may suspend paid features while the dispute is open and will restore them if it is resolved in your favour. None of this affects your right to raise a dispute with your bank, or your rights under section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974.
