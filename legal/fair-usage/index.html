Why this policy exists

Most of what Kemory does costs us almost nothing per user. Capturing your conversations, storing them as memory, searching them, and injecting context back into ChatGPT or Claude all run against your AI subscriptions and your storage allowance. Those are governed by your plan limits, not by this policy.

Two features are different. Ask Kora and Continue with Kora run inference that we pay for. Every time you ask Kora a question, or carry a conversation into Kora from another AI, we send tokens to a model on your behalf and pick up the bill.

We would rather publish honest limits than advertise "unlimited" and quietly throttle you. This policy states what generous individual use looks like, what happens if you go past it, and what we will never do to your data in response.

Units, and what counts

One Ask Kora request is one question and its answer, including any memory retrieval and any follow-up tool calls Kora makes to answer it. A clarifying question you ask in reply is a new request.

One Continue with Kora handoff is one conversation carried into Kora. The handoff includes compaction of the source conversation and the first Kora turn. Every turn after that is an Ask Kora request.

One Counsel query is one question fanned out to multiple models plus the synthesis step. Counsel is counted separately because it costs roughly as much as the number of models convened.

What does not count against your allowance

  • Requests that fail because of an error on our side (5xx), or that we retry ourselves
  • Requests rejected for authentication or permission reasons before any model is called
  • An identical request repeated within 60 seconds where we serve a cached answer
  • Anything you cancel before the first token is returned

A cost backstop

Separately from the counts above, if a single account's inference cost in a calendar month is materially out of line with other accounts on the same plan, we may contact you under the next section. This exists to catch usage patterns the counters do not anticipate, not to claw back normal heavy use.

What happens when you go past an allowance

We escalate in order, and we tell you at every step.

At 80%Of a window or daily allowance, the extension shows a quiet indicator. Nothing changes.
At 100%Ask Kora continues on a lighter open-weight model rather than stopping. You will see which model answered. Continue with Kora and Counsel pause until the window resets.
Sustained overageThree or more consecutive days at 100% — we reduce concurrency and may queue requests behind other users during peak hours.
Persistent overageWe email you. The conversation is about finding the right plan, not about cutting you off. For a heavy Pro user that usually means Team; for a heavy Team account, Enterprise.
AbuseUnder prohibited use, we may throttle, suspend Kora features, or terminate access without notice.
What we will not do

We will not delete, hide, downgrade, or refuse to export your memories because of a usage dispute. Your memory is yours. Capture, search, export and continuing into another AI keep working even if Kora features are paused. This is not negotiable and it survives any future version of this policy.

Prohibited use

The following are outside fair use on every plan, including Enterprise unless your contract says otherwise:

AutomationDriving Ask Kora, Continue with Kora or Counsel with scripts, bots, headless browsers, or scheduled jobs. These features are for a person at a keyboard. Programmatic access belongs on the API, under its own rate limits.
Account sharingMore than one person on a single Free or Pro seat. Team exists for teams.
Reselling or proxyingExposing Kora as an inference endpoint inside your own product, or reselling access.
Training and distillationUsing Kora outputs to train, fine-tune, evaluate or distil a competing model or memory service, or systematically harvesting responses for a dataset.
CircumventionMultiple free accounts to extend allowances, rotating identities, or interfering with metering.
Load that degrades serviceAny pattern that materially affects availability or latency for other users.

Enforcement under this section may be immediate and does not require prior notice.

Model routing

Ask Kora chooses a model per question. Light questions do not go to frontier models, which is how we keep Kora viable at its price. Under sustained load, or once you are past an allowance, routing biases further toward open-weight models we run ourselves.

You will always be able to see which model answered. If you need a specific model for a specific question, use Continue in another AI and run it on your own subscription — your memory travels with you either way.

Changes to this policy

This policy is subject to change. Kemory is in its first release, the underlying model prices move, and these numbers are our best current estimate of generous individual use rather than a settled position. We expect to revise them as we see real usage.

  • Every version is numbered and dated, with a changelog at the foot of this page.
  • Material reductions to allowances on paid plans take effect no earlier than 30 days after we notify account owners by email.
  • Increases, clarifications, and changes to Free plan allowances may take effect immediately.
  • Changes made in response to abuse, security incidents, or provider-side changes outside our control may take effect immediately on any plan.
  • If a material reduction lands mid-term on an annual plan and you would rather not continue, contact us and we will refund the unused portion.

Continued use after a change takes effect is acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions and appeals

If you have been throttled or contacted and you think we have it wrong, reply to the email or write to support@sekondbrain.ai. We would rather move you to the right plan than lose you.

If you are consistently at the ceiling and the next tier does not fit, tell us — an unusual usage pattern is usually a product signal, not a violation.
