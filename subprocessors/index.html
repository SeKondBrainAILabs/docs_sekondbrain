Payments and seller of record

There is no merchant of record. No third party is interposed as the seller of your subscription.

SEKONDBRAIN AI LABS FZCO, our group billing affiliate, is the seller of record for your purchase: it holds the Stripe account, charges your payment method, issues your invoice, and is responsible for applicable taxes on the sale. It is listed above as a sub-processor and acts on our instructions.

Stripe is our payment service provider. It acts as our processor when it executes a payment on our instructions, and separately as an independent controller of transaction data for its own fraud-prevention, anti-money-laundering, sanctions-screening and regulatory-compliance purposes, under Stripe’s own privacy policy. It is not a party to your contract with us and is not the seller of record. See clauses 4.3B and 4.3C of the Terms of Service.

Your payment details are handled by Stripe. We receive transaction confirmations and subscription status, and we do not store full card or bank details.

International transfers

Data at rest is stored in the deployment region selected at organisation creation: Google Cloud Switzerland (rest of world) or Google Cloud India (customers in India). Transfers to Switzerland are covered by UK adequacy regulations.

Billing and account administration data is processed by our group affiliate in the United Arab Emirates. The UAE is not covered by a UK adequacy decision, so that transfer is made under an intra-group data transfer agreement incorporating the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, supported by a transfer risk assessment.

Providers used through global endpoints. Groq, Google (Gemini API), Notion and Twilio SendGrid are used through their global endpoints, which are operated from the United States. We hold no regional residency commitment for those services, so data sent to them may be processed in the United States or in another country in which the provider operates. This applies to conversation content and to any audio or images you send for inference or transcription, to support and feedback records, and to the content of service email.

Stripe’s processing region is not yet stated. It is established by the contracting entity and terms of our own Stripe account rather than by the endpoint we call, and a UAE-domiciled account does not imply processing in the UAE. Until it is confirmed, treat the transfer disclosure for that entry as incomplete.

Transfers to India, to the UAE, to the United States, and any other restricted transfer — including processing by inference, transcription and email providers in other jurisdictions — are made under the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, with supplementary measures where appropriate. You may request details of the safeguards applicable to a specific transfer at privacy@sekondbrain.ai.
